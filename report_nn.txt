Three regimes (hit source varies, all else fixed): our StereoTracking sim material OFF, our sim
material ON, and FairShip's OWN digitized daughter hits (real spectrometer field map + geometry
calibrated from the MC hit cloud).
Columns: R=retina N=NLL | Tu=tubes(V1, wire centre) TD=TDC(V2, raw readout, network proxy)
         Dr=drift(V3, smeared r_drift) Hi=hits(V4, exact x,y, sanity floor).  +FairShip = its reco.
Cuts: OUR trackers use OUR cuts (>=25 hits/track, >=3 stations, chi2/Ndf<4, DoCA<=2cm, from our
      fit); the FairShip column uses FAIRSHIP cuts (events FairShip reconstructed). 'acceptance %'
      = fraction passing; all other rows are on each column's accepted subset.
Table 1 rows: dp/|p| (median/RMS %); p_x/y/z & vertex_x/y/z (median|err|, RMSE; GeV/cm); doca cm.
Table 2 rows: per-component R^2 = 1 - MSE/Var(true) (1=perfect, 0=predict-the-mean, <0=worse).
Fit on the first 16384 events. Regimes done: 3/3 (COMPLETE).
Network columns (this report): NN = the trained regressor under OUR cuts (it has no per-track fit,
  so it borrows the nll-tdc tracker's accepted subset); NN-FS = the same network under FAIRSHIP cuts.
  The network is fed its NATIVE realistic fired-straw hits (re-simulated for the sim regimes; FairShip's
  own digitized hits for the FairShip regime -- a domain shift, so NN/NN-FS there are an OOD estimate).
